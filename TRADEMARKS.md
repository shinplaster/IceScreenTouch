# IceScreen Touch name and icon

The source code of IceScreen Touch is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The MIT License covers the code; it does not give you the right to use the name "IceScreen Touch" or the IceScreen Touch icon as the name or mark of your own product.

The name and the icon tell people that a build is the official one from the IceScreen Touch project (published by Shin-plaster).

## You may, without asking

- Build and use IceScreen Touch for yourself, and change it as you like.
- Fork the repository and send changes back.
- Say that your work is "based on IceScreen Touch" or "a fork of IceScreen Touch", and refer to IceScreen Touch by name when you write about it.

## Please do not

- Share or sell a build (free or paid, on a store, a website or elsewhere) under the name "IceScreen Touch", a confusingly similar name, or with the IceScreen Touch icon.
- Suggest that your build or product is official, or endorsed by the IceScreen Touch project.

If you share a build, give it a different name and icon, and say that it is not the official IceScreen Touch.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/shinplaster/IceScreenTouch/issues.
